APNA ID PRO - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 September 2026

APNA ID PRO ("we", "our", or "the App") is an ID card management application designed for authorised owners, distributors, schools, colleges, and institutions to manage student information and generate ID cards.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed through APNA ID PRO, why it is used, how it is protected, and the choices available to users.

1. Information We May Process

Depending on how the App is used, APNA ID PRO may process:

  • Account and authentication information such as email address, username, and account role.
  • School, college, institution, distributor, and owner information entered into the App.
  • Student information entered for ID card creation, which may include name, admission/roll number, class, date of birth, mobile number, address, and other fields provided by an authorised user.
  • Student photographs or other images uploaded for ID cards.
  • ID card template, generated ID, and related application data.
  • Information voluntarily provided when contacting support.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed through the App may be used to:

  • Create and authenticate authorised user accounts.
  • Manage schools, distributors, classes, students, and ID card records.
  • Generate, preview, save, and print ID cards and related documents.
  • Provide application functionality and technical support.
  • Maintain the security and integrity of the service.

3. Data Storage and Service Providers

APNA ID PRO uses Supabase services for application backend, authentication, database, and storage functionality. Information submitted through the App may therefore be processed and stored using these services.

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent users' personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate the App, such as backend, authentication, database, and storage providers.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law, legal process, or to protect the security, rights, or property of the service and its users.

5. User Responsibility and Authorised Access

APNA ID PRO may be used by authorised owners, distributors, schools, colleges, or institutions. Users are responsible for ensuring that they have the appropriate authority and lawful basis to enter and manage student or other personal information in the App.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the service, maintain authorised account and ID card records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

An authorised account holder may request deletion of applicable information by contacting us. Certain information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security and operational purposes.

8. Children's Privacy

APNA ID PRO is intended for authorised adult users such as school, college, institution, distributor, or owner accounts. The App is not intended to be used independently by children to create their own accounts.

Authorised institutions may enter student information for legitimate educational or administrative purposes and are responsible for obtaining any permissions required by applicable law.

9. Advertising

The current version of APNA ID PRO does not display advertising.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, its services, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
